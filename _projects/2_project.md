Date: July 2026 - August 2026

Problem: Solved an unmonitored door access safety issue by designing and building a self-closing door mechanism from scratch.

Solution: CAD'd, prototyped, and mounted a piston actuated system with a timing relay and limit switch, ensuring the door automatically closes and locks after a set time interval, eliminating the risk of the room being left accessible to unauthorized entry.
